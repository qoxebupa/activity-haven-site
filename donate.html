Why People Give

People donate to Activity Haven because they are investing in something much bigger than a recreation centre — they are helping older adults stay active, connected, healthy, and independent.

Activity Haven is a registered not-for-profit. Donations help fill the gap between membership and program fees and the true cost of delivering high-quality recreation, wellness, educational, and social opportunities. Every contribution — large or small — helps keep the doors open, programs thriving, and older adults connected to the community they call home.

"Your donation is more than a gift — it's an investment in healthy aging, friendship, and community. Together, we're creating a community where everyone can age with dignity, purpose, and joy."
