Who we are

Our club gives our members a place to belong.

To be with people their own age, to share their interests and their talents, to have fun, to laugh, and to enjoy themselves. We're happy you're here.

Vision

A centre that is proactive, progressive, accountable and innovative, providing recreational services to the 50+ population in Peterborough and surrounding areas.

Mission

Promoting socialization of adults 50+ to support and strengthen health and wellbeing — through intellectual, physical, creative and social engagement.

Values

Involvement, innovation, integrity, transparency and inclusiveness are foundational to everything we do.
