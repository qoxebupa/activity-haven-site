About Activity Haven

Serving adults 50+ in Peterborough since 1990.

Who We Are

Our club gives our members a place to belong, to be with people their own age and to share their interests and their talents, to have fun, to laugh and to enjoy themselves. We're happy you're here!

Our Story

In April 1990, Activity Haven Recreation Centre of Peterborough became an incorporated organization and operates independently under the guidance of a Board of Directors. In 1994, with the help of infrastructure funds, the club was able to increase our facilities — including making areas handicap accessible.

Today we offer more than 80 programs ongoing in our rooms at the Queen Alexandra Building, from Euchre and Bridge to Line Dancing, Woodcarving, Tai Chi, and instructor-led yoga five days a week. Our club could not function without our wonderful volunteers — over 200 of them help out across every area of the club.

Vision

A centre that is proactive, progressive, accountable and innovative, providing recreational services to the 50+ population in Peterborough and surrounding areas.

Mission

To promote socialization of adults 50+ with a view to support and strengthen the determinants of health and wellbeing — encouraging our members through intellectual, physical, creative and social engagement to be active and involved, develop friendships, and maintain an independent, healthy lifestyle.

Values

Involvement, innovation, integrity, transparency and inclusiveness are foundational to every aspect of the centre.
