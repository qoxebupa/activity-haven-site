      How the Hub is read

      • Reads the Hub every time the page opens, and again every 5 minutes. No copying needed.
      • Rows with anything in Status (Past, Ended) are left out. Dated events that have passed are left out too.
      • "RM 9/3" means normal room / no-elevator room. The Elevator switch picks which one is shown (* on the block).
      • Room 5 can be split: write 5 inner or 5 outer in the Room cell. Inner and outer don't clash with each other; plain 5 means the whole room.
      • Adding shared to the Room cell (e.g. 5 shared) means it may share with another "shared" booking, so it's shown but not flagged as a clash.
      • Times with no end (e.g. "1:00") are assumed to run 2 hours (faint stripes). Times without am/pm: 8–11 morning, 12 noon, 1–7 afternoon.
