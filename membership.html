Becoming a Member

Thank you for your interest in becoming a member at Activity Haven! Here's what you need to know:

  • A membership costs $30 for the year, valid for one year from your purchase date.
  • You must be a member to participate in Instructional & Drop-in Programs (some special events are an exception).
  • There is a $3 user fee for each program you attend at Activity Haven.
  • Punch cards can be purchased in the office in $15 or $30 denominations — a $15 card gets you 5 uses, and a $30 card gets you 10 uses.

Ready to join, or have a question first? Drop by the office or reach out — we're happy to help you get started.
